SENTINEL-AI is a reliability-aware, context-conditioned surveillance model. It fuses face, pose, motion and scene metadata through a learned attention gate — and drops any signal it can't trust.

Every alert is scored by a closed-form objective. Every attribution is written in English. Every incident is logged and grounded against a retrieval index.

// SECTION 03 — method

One gate, one fusion, one written objective.

SENTINEL-AI does not run an ensemble of tricks. It runs a single, written process. Every branch emits an embedding and a self-reported reliability score. The attention gate closes over anything it can't trust — occluded faces, missing landmarks, black frames — and re-normalises the rest.

The fusion head is temperature-calibrated so the anomaly probability is a probability, not a rank. When it fires, the RAG engine grounds the alert against 1 400 historical incidents and SHAP explains, in English, which modality carried the decision.

// SECTION 04

How the frame becomes an alert.

Four steps · per frame
STEP 1

Read the scene

OpenCV runs multi-scale Haar cascades with CLAHE and NMS on every frame; MediaPipe emits 33 3-D pose landmarks; scene metadata is normalised against the zone baseline.

STEP 2

Weight the evidence

Each branch reports an embedding and a reliability α. Occluded faces and dropped keypoints self-report low α and are demoted — never silently trusted.

STEP 3

Fuse & calibrate

The 224-D concatenated vector passes the attention head. Temperature scaling produces a genuine 5-class anomaly probability across Normal, Fall, Fighting, Panic and Loitering.

STEP 4

Ground & explain

If risk crosses threshold, TF-IDF retrieves the 5 nearest historical incidents. SHAP names the modalities that carried the alarm. The alert is written in one sentence of English.
